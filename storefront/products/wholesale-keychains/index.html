BEFORE YOU ORDER

little details.

Ask us a question ↗
Where do you ship?

We ship via UPS across the United States and to Puerto Rico. Shipping is $4.99, and free on orders of $50 or more. A tracking number is included.

How long will my order take?

Personalized products take up to 2 days to make. Delivery takes another 3–5 days after the piece is ready.

Can I pick up my order?

Local pickup is available in Katy, TX 77449. Contact us to arrange the location and time.

Can I return a personalized piece?

Personalized designs are not eligible for returns. Double-check the spelling and your selections before ordering. No separate design proof is sent before production.
